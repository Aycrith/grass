> Goal: 1 in 10 yards signs generates a phone call or GBP tap.

## Specs

- **Size:** 18" × 24" (standard yard sign)
- **Material:** 4mm coroplast (lightweight, weatherproof)
- **Stake:** H-stake wire, 30" tall
- **Sides:** Two-sided (so neighbors see it from either direction)

## Why this works

- **"Just finished here"** is the proof-of-work signal. Owner walks out the next morning, sees the result, sees the sign.
- **Front is brand.** Back is conversion. Symmetric design.
- **Phone + URL + QR** — three ways to convert, by preference.
- **Bottom strip = trust signals.** Free quote. No contract.

## Sign discipline

- **Drop the sign at job start.** Take it down at job end if owner asks; otherwise leave 24-48h.
- **Always get permission** before placing. "Want a yard sign for 48 hours? Helps the neighbors see the work." 95% say yes.
- **Move the sign between yards** if doing multiple jobs in a day. Don't leave it planted if the yard is small.
- **Replace if faded or bent.** Cheap signs look cheap.

## Theft / vandalism

- Sign theft happens ~5% of the time. Don't chase it.
- Vandalism (spray paint, bent stake) happens <1%. Treat it as the cost of being visible.
- Don't print anything on the sign that would make you feel exposed if vandalized.
