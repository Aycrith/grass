# Phone Scripts — Inbound + Outbound + Weather Cancel

> **Purpose:** Three scripts covering every phone interaction in Year 1.
> Voice per `brand/guidelines.md` — plain, honest, local, warm, practical.
> **Tone:** Friendly-neighbor, not sales-corporate.
> **Format:** Spoken aloud. Brackets = variable. Avoid jargon.

---

## SCRIPT 1 — Inbound: New Customer Call

**Use when:** Someone calls from a yard sign, NextDoor, Google, or referral.
**Length target:** 2-3 minutes. Don't oversell. They called you; they're already warm.

```
[Pick up by 3rd ring. Smile when you answer — it changes your voice.]

"Thanks for calling Largo Lawn, this is [your first name]. How can I help?"

[Listen. Don't interrupt. Let them talk for 30-60 seconds. They will tell you:
 - What they want (mow, edge, mulch, etc.)
 - Lot size or address (rough — "standard Pinellas lot, half acre-ish?")
 - How often they're hoping (weekly, bi-weekly, one-time)
 - Anything else on their mind (gate code, dogs, prior bad experience)]

"Got it. So a [bi-weekly mow, edge, and blow] on a [half-acre lot] in
[33773]. Free quote, right? What's the best address for me to look at?"

[Take address. Confirm phone number and best email.]

"I can have a quote back to you within 24 hours — usually same-day if I
can swing by this afternoon. Two questions to help me quote:

1. Is the lot flat or does it slope / have fences or tight gates?
2. Anything I should know about the yard — wet spots, irrigation lines,
   recent work by someone else?

[Take notes. Confirm.]

"You'll get an email or text from me by [tomorrow morning / end of day]
with a flat quote — no surprise fees. If you want to go ahead, just
reply 'yes' and we'll get you on the schedule.

Anything else?"

[If they ask pricing on the phone:]

"For a standard half-acre bi-weekly mow, you're looking at $[X-Y]
per visit, depending on grass length and edge work. I'll send you the
exact quote in writing once I've seen the lot."

[Close warmly.]

"Great. Talk soon. Thanks for calling."

[Hang up. Within 15 minutes, add to CRM with call timestamp + notes.]
```

### Common follow-ups during the call

| Customer asks | You say |
|---|---|
| "Are you licensed?" | "Not yet — we're building toward that milestone this year. Until then we operate with hand tools only and a signed waiver. I'll be transparent about this every time it matters." |
| "Do you have insurance?" | "Not yet — same answer as licensed. Insurance is queued for when revenue supports it. We limit scope to small lots with hand tools in the meantime." |
| "How soon can you start?" | "First visit within 3-5 business days of quote acceptance, weather permitting. Spring and hurricane season stretch that to 7-10 days." |
| "What if it rains?" | "We don't mow wet grass — it tears the turf. We auto-reschedule to the next clear day at no charge. You'll get a text by 6:30 AM if we're pushing the visit." |
| "Can you do it today?" | "Depends on where you are and what's already on the route. What's the address?" |
| "Can you give me a ballpark on the phone?" | (see pricing line above) |

## SCRIPT 2 — Outbound: Personal-Network Pilot Outreach

**Use when:** First 5 paid pilots come from your personal network. Text, don't call (less intrusive; they respond on their schedule).
**Length target:** 2-3 sentences. No pressure.
**Send to:** 5-10 neighbors, family, friends in 33771 + adjacent ZIPs.

```
Text template (replace [bracketed] bits):

Hey [name] — I'm getting a lawn-care side business off the ground
in Largo. Doing a few free pilot jobs this month to build reviews.
[Your address] is on my route — want me to mow your [front / back /
whole] yard this week? Free, no obligation. Just want a 5-star
Google review if you're happy with it.

Either way, appreciate it.
```

### Why text and not call

- Calls at 6pm = intrusion. Texts = they respond on their time.
- Texts convert at ~40% for "free pilot" offers. Calls convert at ~15%.
- Texts leave a paper trail in your messages app for the post-mortem.

### If they say yes

1. Schedule within 3-5 days.
2. Show up 15 min early. Walk the lot with them.
3. Do the work.
4. At completion, hand them the review-magnet card.
5. Send a follow-up text: *"How'd it look? Mind dropping a quick Google review? [link]"*

### If they say no

1. "No worries. If you ever need a hand, you know where to find me."
2. Add them to your "warm referral" list — they may say yes in 6 months.

### If they don't reply within 7 days

- One follow-up: *"Hey [name] — saw you didn't see my last text. No pressure at all, just circling back. If now isn't a good time, totally fine."*
- If still no reply in 7 more days: stop. Add to "cold" list.

## SCRIPT 3 — Weather Cancellation (Customer-Facing Text)

**Use when:** Forecast shows >50% rain on a scheduled visit day.
**Send by:** 6:30 AM the day of (texts sent earlier feel spammy; texts sent at 7:30 catch them at the door).
**Length:** 1 sentence + reschedule offer.

```
[Day-of-cancel template]

Hey [first name] — rain's coming through today so we're pushing your
[mow / visit] to [next clear day]. No charge for the reschedule.
You'll get a text tomorrow morning confirming the new time.
```

### If they're home and see you didn't show

- They'll call or text. Reply within 15 minutes.
- "Hey [name] — saw your message. We pushed the visit because of the
  rain — sorry for the surprise. New time is [X]. Want me to call you
  when I'm on the way?"

### If you can squeeze them in same day between rain bands

- Text by 11 AM: "Rain cleared faster than expected — I can fit your
  mow in this afternoon around [time]. Want it, or push to [tomorrow]?"
- 50/50 they'll say yes. Either answer is fine.

## General phone principles

1. **Answer by the 3rd ring.** Voice mail is a missed-lead killer.
2. **Smile when you answer.** It changes your tone audibly.
3. **Don't sell.** Answer questions, take notes, send a quote.
4. **Never quote a number on the phone without seeing the lot.** Always: "depends on lot size — I'll quote in writing within 24 hours."
5. **Follow up the same day.** Every. Single. Time.
6. **Document every call.** Even 30-second calls. CRM entry within 15 minutes.
