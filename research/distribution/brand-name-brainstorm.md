## Verification protocol (BEFORE selecting any name)

For every candidate that survives the conceptual filter:

1. **Domain check** (Cloudflare Registrar OR Porkbun): verify the
   `.com` is unregistered. Try in this order:
   - `<name>.com`
   - `<name>.net`
2. **USPTO TESS live search** at https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/:
   - Class 037 (building maintenance / landscaping)
   - Class 044 (lawn care / agricultural)
3. **Sunbiz search** at https://search.sunbiz.org/ for FL fictitious
   name conflicts.
4. **Social handle spot-check:**
   - Instagram `<name>` handle availability
   - Facebook Page `<name>` availability (less critical — name ≠ handle)
5. **Phone-spell test:** say it out loud, 5 times, to a 5-year-old
   in another room. If they can spell it back, it passes.

## Decision (steward call)

Select 1 from the combined ~17-candidate pool. Factors:

- **Your surname starts with H/K/C?** The family-name angles
  (HollisLawn, KearnsYard, CortezLawn) become even more defensible
  because they signal personal accountability.
- **You plan to stay 33771-only?** The Largo-prefixed angles
  (LargoYardPro, LargoLawns) are slightly stronger SEO.
- **You want room to expand to all Pinellas later?** PinellasLawn.com
  scales without rename.

Default recommendation: **HollisLawn.com** at $9.73/yr on Porkbun
(unless you want Cloudflare at $9.15 — equivalent). If that name
doesn't resonate personally, fall to **LargoYardPro.com**.
