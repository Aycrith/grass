## Context

Brand + domain are customer-facing assets. They need to:
- Be available (not trademarked by a competitor, especially locally).
- Communicate the service line (landscaping / lawn care).
- Rank for service-area keywords (Largo, FL).
- Be pronounceable, spellable, and memorable on phone calls.

## Decision (Phase A — strategy)

**Strategy: Local-keyword-anchored brand name + matching .com domain, with a defensible trademark search before commitment.**

### Naming framework

| Pattern | Example | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| [City] + [Service] | LargoLawnCo | Hyperlocal, instant SEO signal | Generic; hard to trademark |
| [Place feature] + [Service] | BayAreaLawns | Evokes geography; Tampa Bay brand-able | Less specific to 33771 |
| [Personal] + [Service] | CameronsLawn | Personal accountability signal; owner can become brand | Personal brand lock-in |
| Abstract + .co | Greenwise | Memorable, brandable | No local SEO signal in name |
| Hybrid | TampaBayLawnPros | Combines region + service + trust | Long domain |

### Recommended path

**For Mission 1, prefer a [Place feature] + [Service] hybrid that:**
1. Spells correctly on first read.
2. Has an available .com domain (NOT .net, NOT .lawncare, NOT .services — those dilute brand).
3. Is available as a Florida fictitious name (Sunbiz Fictitious Name Registration, $50).
4. Is clear of any US trademark (search USPTO TESS).
5. GBP-categorizable as "Lawn care service" (NOT "Landscaper" — categorical SEO difference, see `research/seo/largo-keyword-map.md`).
6. Has the matching handle on Nextdoor, Facebook, Instagram, GBP, Yelp.

### Domain acquisition rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| .com only | Other TLDs reduce direct traffic and look unprofessional. |
| ≤14 characters | Easy to spell on phone; fits in GBP name (60 char limit). |
| No hyphens | Customer confusion on phone. |
| No numbers | Hard to spell, looks spammy. |
| Available in all major social platforms | Brand consistency. |
| Cheap acquisition | $10-15/yr max — anything higher is a red flag. |

## Phase B (pending — needs steward action)

Once the steward picks 3-5 candidate names from the framework above, execute:

1. **Domain availability check** — Namecheap, Google Domains, GoDaddy.
2. **USPTO TESS search** — trademark clearance for each candidate.
3. **Sunbiz fictitious name search** — confirm not already registered in FL.
4. **Social handle check** — namechk.com or similar.
5. **Phone-spell test** — say the name out loud to 3 friends; can they spell it back?
6. **Decision matrix** — pick top 1, commit.

## Trademark watch

- **DO NOT** use names that include "Tampa Bay" unless trademark clearance is done (Tampa Bay Buccaneers, Tampa Bay Lightning hold broader marks).
- **DO NOT** use words like "Premier," "Elite," "Best," "Top" — generic and hard to defend.
- **Avoid geographic descriptors that competitors may claim** ("Largo" alone may be tricky if a future "Largo Premier Lawns" wants to register).

## Risks accepted

- **Brand-name typo squatting.** Domains like [brand]lawn.com or [brand]lawncare.com may be squatted. Mitigation: buy defensively ($100-200 total).
- **TESS clearance gap.** USPTO search is best-effort, not exhaustive. Mitigation: monitor trademark filings annually.
- **Personal brand risk.** If the brand is personal ("Cameron's Lawn"), the steward IS the brand; selling the business later is harder. Mitigation: keep entity distinct from brand from Day 1.
