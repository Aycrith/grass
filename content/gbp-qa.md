## Q1: "Do you mow in the rain?"

**A:** No — wet mowing causes clumping, ruts, and torn turf. If your scheduled day is rained out, we auto-reschedule to the next clear day at no charge. You don't have to call us; we watch the radar and text you by 6:30 AM if we're pushing the visit.

## Q2: "How often should I mow in Florida?"

**A:** During the growing season (April–October), weekly mowing is ideal. November–March, bi-weekly is usually enough. The honest answer depends on your grass type (St. Augustine vs. Bahia vs. Zoysia), irrigation, and how short you like it. We recommend a schedule at quote-time and adjust after the first month if growth rate surprises us.

## Q3: "Are you licensed and insured?"

**A:** Not yet — and we won't say we are until we are. Insurance binding is queued for the $2,500 cumulative-cash milestone (currently $0). Until then, we operate under a signed waiver-of-liability on every quote, hand tools only, lots ≤0.25 acre. We'll update this Q&A the day our GL policy binds.

## Q4: "What if I have a locked gate or dogs?"

**A:** We note gate codes and dog status in your property file at quote-time. Every visit, the crew is briefed before arrival. If your dog is reactive, please tell us at quote-time — we may reschedule around a day the dog is inside.

## Q5: "Do you offer one-time mows or only recurring?"

**A:** Both. Recurring (weekly or bi-weekly) is the better value — you save ~15% per visit and we keep your yard on a consistent schedule. One-time mows are $65-95 depending on lot size. Most recurring customers convert after a single one-time pilot.

## Q6: "What areas do you serve?"

**A:** Six Pinellas County ZIPs: 33771 (Largo), 33770 (Largo), 33773 (Largo), 33774 (Largo), 33778 (Largo), 33756 (Clearwater). If you're just outside this footprint, ask — we sometimes accept adjacent customers on the route home.

## Q7: "How is hurricane season handled?"

**A:** June 1 – November 30. When a named storm is forecast within 48 hours and Pinellas is in the cone, OR when sustained winds hit 30+ mph locally, we pause regular scheduling and switch to hurricane mode: prep visits (securing loose items, removing vulnerable branches) and post-storm cleanup. We do NOT work outdoors during sustained 30+ mph winds. Outdoor work resumes 24 hours after the storm passes and conditions are safe.

## Q8: "Do you take debit/credit cards?"

**A:** Yes — we invoice via email with a Stripe "Pay Now" link. Most customers pay within 24 hours of the invoice landing. Cash, check, Cash App, Venmo, and Zelle also accepted.

## Q9: "Do you pull weeds?"

**A:** Hand-pulling is included in bed-edging visits. Heavy weed control (especially in St. Augustine or Bermuda lawns) is a different service — ask us about it at quote-time and we'll quote honestly. We don't spray herbicide without the FDACS Limited Commercial Fertilizer Applicator license, which we don't currently hold.

## Q10: "Can you edge along pavers without damaging them?"

**A:** Yes — we adjust the edger depth to match the paver height. For irregular natural stone, we recommend bed-line edging instead (a defined cut between stone and turf).

## Q11: "Will you match a competitor's price?"

**A:** We won't race to the bottom. Our pricing is built on cost-of-service + a fair solo-operator margin, and we're transparent about it. If a competitor is cheaper, they're either cutting corners (subcontracting, no-shows, unverified credentials) or underquoting to land the job. We'd rather lose the job than race on price.

## Q12: "How quickly can you start?"

**A:** Most quotes are returned within 24 hours during business days. First visit is typically within 3-5 business days of quote acceptance, weather permitting. Hurricane season and spring growth surges can extend this to 7-10 days — we'll tell you up front if we're scheduling out.
