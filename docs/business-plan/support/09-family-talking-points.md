## 1. Call structure (15 minutes)

| Minutes | Topic | Founder script |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Welcome, thanks | "Hi, thanks so much for taking the time. I really appreciate it." |
| 2–5 | What Largo Lawn does | "I want to walk you through what I'm planning. It's a small lawn-care business in Largo, Florida. I mow lawns for local families — about 50 of them, mostly weekly. I do all the work myself." |
| 5–8 | Use of funds + return | "I need $15,000 to get started. Most of that — about $9,000 — is for equipment: a mower, a trimmer, a blower, a trailer. The rest covers insurance, software, marketing, and a small working capital reserve. In return, you'd own about 9% of the business. You'd get monthly updates and quarterly statements." |
| 8–11 | Risk + downside | "I want to be honest. The business may not work. It may take longer to find customers than I expect. Equipment can break. Weather can interrupt. The $15,000 may not be returned quickly, and there's a chance some or all could be lost." |
| 11–13 | Q&A | "What questions do you have?" |
| 13–14 | Next steps | "Take a few days to think about it. I'll send you a follow-up email tomorrow. If you want to talk more, just let me know." |
| 14–15 | Close | "Thanks again. I really appreciate your consideration." |

## 2. Lead-with topics (in order)

1. **What Largo Lawn does.** Weekly residential lawn care in Largo FL, 1/4-acre average lot, $48/week.
2. **Why customers would pay.** Recurring reliability (same day, same guy, every week), no contract, local operator who picks up the phone.
3. **What the founder will personally do.** All work, day 1. 30–40 hrs/wk through M6; 30 hrs/wk after PMF.
4. **How much money is needed and exactly where it goes.** $15,000 seed. Equipment $9K, insurance $330, licensing $310, software $470, marketing $3,500, reserve $1,400.
5. **What success looks like in Year 1.** $62,100 gross revenue (range $30,192–$106,560); $16,590 net profit (range $7,800–$44,000); 25 customers by M6; 45 by M12.
6. **What could go wrong.** Weather, equipment, slow customer acquisition, founder availability.
7. **What the investor can expect.** Monthly 1-page email, quarterly P&L, annual review meeting; distributions start Month 6–9 if business succeeds.
