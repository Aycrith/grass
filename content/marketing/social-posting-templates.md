## WHAT THESE POSTS DO (AND DON'T)

**Do:** Generate raw inbound at near-zero cost.

**Don't:** Stay posted once the lead flow is healthy. Facebook Marketplace "Services" listings get stale after 7-10 days. Re-post with new photos every other week during growth phase.

**Don't:** Spam. NextDoor in particular penalizes over-posting. Maximum 1 post/zip/month.

**Don't:** Neglect replies. Response time is the #1 conversion factor in local service businesses. Reply within 1 hour during business hours; 4 hours overnight.
