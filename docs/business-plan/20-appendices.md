## 2. Full glossary (alphabetical)

| Term | Plain-language definition |
|---|---|
| **ADR** | Architecture Decision Record. A document that captures a decision, its rationale, alternatives, and trade-offs. |
| **AFR** | Applicable Federal Rate. The IRS interest rate used for below-market loans to avoid gift-tax issues. |
| **Agent (GRASS)** | An autonomous AI function within the GRASS organization, scoped to a single division (research, architecture, engineering, etc.). 13 agents defined. |
| **ARR** | Annual Recurring Revenue. Yearly revenue extrapolated from current monthly recurring revenue. |
| **BTR** | Business Tax Receipt. A local business license, required in Florida counties and municipalities. |
| **Cap table** | Capitalization table. A table showing who owns what % of the business. |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost. How much it costs to get one new customer. |
| **Cumulative cash** | Running total of cash generated by the business from inception, used to trigger cash-ladder gates in D-0011. |
| **D-####** | Decision record ID. Format: `D-NNNN-short-slug.md` in `governance/decisions/`. |
| **DR-1** | Florida Department of Revenue application for sales tax registration. |
| **DWC-250** | Florida workers' compensation exemption form. Required for sole proprietors with no employees. |
| **EBITDA** | Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. Standard profitability metric. |
| **EIN** | Employer Identification Number. Federal tax ID for a business. |
| **Equity** | Ownership. If you buy 15% equity, you own 15% of the business. |
| **Forecast** | A prediction based on current information. Not a guarantee. |
| **Founder** | Cameron Pike. The sole human operator of Largo Lawn and steward of GRASS. |
| **Founding team** | The founder plus the 13 GRASS AI agents. |
| **GBP** | Google Business Profile. The Google listing that shows up when customers search "lawn care near me." |
| **GL insurance** | General Liability insurance. Covers damage or injury to customers or the public. |
| **GRASS** | The autonomous AI organization that runs Largo Lawn and prepares for future missions. Internal name; not in family materials. |
| **Hard rule** | A constitutional rule that cannot be overridden except by charter amendment. |
| **K-1** | IRS Schedule K-1. A tax form showing an investor's share of business income. |
| **Largo Lawn** | The Mission 1 lawn care business operating in Largo, FL 33771. Future trade name of FL LLC. |
| **LLC** | Limited Liability Company. A legal structure that separates personal money from business money. |
| **Loan (family)** | A 0% interest loan from a family member to the founder. Not equity; no board seat. |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value. How much money one customer pays over the time they stay a customer. |
| **MFI** | Multi-Factor Identification. A security check requiring more than one piece of evidence. |
| **Mission 1** | The first production business the GRASS organization is launching. (Largo Lawn.) |
| **Mission 2** | The next business, not yet launched. Pool, pressure wash, pet waste are candidates. |
| **MFN** | Most Favored Nation. A clause that ensures an early investor gets terms at least as good as later investors. |
| **MRR** | Monthly Recurring Revenue. Monthly revenue from subscriptions/contracts. |
| **NALP** | National Association of Landscape Professionals. Industry trade body. |
| **Pilot Exception** | Charter amendment allowing mission work in parallel with phase exit gates under strict audit. |
| **Post-money valuation** | What the business is worth after the new investment. Pre-money + new money = post-money. |
| **Pre-money valuation** | What the business is worth before the new investment. |
| **Preflight** | Validation script that runs before any send. Blocks on any failure. |
| **PRP** | Pattern-Reference-Plan. A structured plan template in the GRASS spec-driven framework. |
| **Reserve** | Money kept aside for unexpected costs or slow months. |
| **Revenue Share Agreement (RSA)** | An investment instrument where the investor receives a percentage of monthly revenue up to a cap. |
| **ROFR** | Right of First Refusal. The right to match an offer before the seller can accept a third party. |
| **RSA** | See Revenue Share Agreement. |
| **SAFE** | Simple Agreement for Future Equity. A standard YC investment instrument. |
| **SBA** | Small Business Administration. US federal agency supporting small businesses. |
| **SCORE** | Service Corps of Retired Executives. SBA partner providing free mentorship to small businesses. |
| **SDE** | Seller's Discretionary Earnings. Profit metric that adds back owner compensation. |
| **Seed investment** | Money given to start or grow a small business. The investor becomes a partial owner. |
| **Snapshot** | A point-in-time archive of artifacts. Stored in `output/snapshots/<timestamp>/`. |
| **Sunbiz** | Florida's online business registration system (sunbiz.org). |
| **T12M** | Trailing 12 Months. The most recent 12-month period. |
| **Working capital** | Money available for day-to-day operations. |
| **Y1** | Year 1. The first 12 months of operations. |
