## 6. Financial Requirements

### 6.1 Recommended Ask

| Tier | Amount | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Lean launch | **$10,000** | Used equipment, insurance, minimal working capital |
| **Recommended** | **$12,000** | Adds buffer and hurricane reserve |
| Growth runway | **$15,000** | Adds larger buffer and faster equipment replacement |

**Primary ask:** **$12,000** at 0% interest, 24-month term, family loan.

### 6.2 Anxiety-Lowering Arrangements

The v3.0 plan must explicitly propose terms that lower the lender's risk:

1. **0% interest** — no imputed-interest filing burden (below $19K gift exclusion).
2. **Personal guarantee by founder** — founder stands behind the loan.
3. **Milestone-based release** — funds released in tranches tied to gates, not upfront lump sum:
   - Tranche 1: $3,000 at launch (LLC, insurance, first equipment)
   - Tranche 2: $4,000 at Month 3 (3 paying customers, BTRs filed)
   - Tranche 3: $5,000 at Month 6 (15 customers, GL insurance bound)
4. **Early repayment option** — founder can repay early without penalty.
5. **Monthly 1-page update** — founder sends a short update with customer count, cash position, and next milestone.
6. **Pause / restructuring clause** — if the business hits the pessimistic scenario (≤24 customers by Month 12), the repayment schedule is renegotiated before any default.

### 6.3 Sensitivity Analysis

v3.0 must include **two full sensitivity matrices** to show the lender how robust the plan is under stress.

#### Matrix 1: Revenue drivers

Rows: new customers per month. Columns: weekly price + retention mix. Each cell shows Year 1 net profit.

| New customers / mo | $40/wk, 70% retention | $48/wk, 80% retention | $56/wk, 85% retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.0 (pessimistic) | $3,200 | $5,100 | $6,800 |
| 3.75 (baseline) | $9,400 | **$16,590** | $22,100 |
| 5.0 (stretch) | $18,500 | $32,700 | $44,000 |

> The baseline remains profitable even at a lower price and lower retention.

#### Matrix 2: Capital & cost drivers

Rows: loan amount. Columns: post-credit CAC + founder wage assumption. Each cell shows Year 2 net profit.

| Loan amount | $90 CAC, no founder wage | $145 CAC, $25/hr wage | $200 CAC, $30/hr wage |
|---|---|---|---|
| $10,000 | $22,100 | $18,400 | $14,200 |
| $12,000 | $21,800 | $18,100 | $13,900 |
| $15,000 | $21,500 | $17,800 | $13,600 |

> The $12,000 loan is the recommended balance between runway and Year 2 cash flow.
