## Why this design works

- **Visual proof of work.** "Just finished next door" is the single best sales line in lawn care.
- **QR code front and center.** Smartphone-first; takes 5 seconds.
- **Specific services listed, not corporate fluff.** No "premier landscaping solutions."
- **"No contract" is a trust signal** in a market where most operators lock you in.

## Drop strategy

- **Drop only after paid work** in the neighborhood. Otherwise it's spam.
- **Within 24 hours of completion.** Owner notices the work, sees the hanger, remembers.
- **Skip the immediate neighbors** (they saw you working; no need to convince them).
- **Walk a 0.5-mile radius from the completed job.** Most lead-conversion comes from people who saw the result in person.
- **250 hangers per quarter is plenty.** Order once, restock when low.

## Conversion math

- 250 hangers × 0.5-mile radius = ~80 unique households.
- 1-2% conversion = 1-2 new quotes per drop.
- 60% quote-to-job = ~1 new paying customer per drop.
- $1,780 Year 1 LTV per customer = **$1,780 return on a $35 spend = 51× ROI.**
