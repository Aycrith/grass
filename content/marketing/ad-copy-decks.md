## What to measure (per platform)

- **Cost per lead (CPL):** Total spend ÷ form submissions
- **Cost per qualified lead (CPQL):** Total spend ÷ leads in service area
- **Cost per appointment (CPA):** Total spend ÷ booked appointments
- **Cost per closed job (CPCJ):** Total spend ÷ paid jobs

**Target CPL:** < $25 for lawn care in 33771 (industry benchmark).
**Target CPCJ:** < $50.

If CPL > $40 in any campaign after 2 weeks, pause it. Free credits only.
