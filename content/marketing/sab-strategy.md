# Service-Area Business (SAB) Address Strategy

> **When this matters:** before creating the GBP profile (OBJ-M2-006).
> GBP requires a business address. Two options: **visible address** vs.
> **service-area business** (SAB, address hidden). This doc recommends one,
> lists the trade-offs, and gives the actions.

---

## TL;DR — recommendation

**Use a service-area business (SAB) profile with the founder's residential
address as the registration-only address, served from a Largo PO box if
possible.** Hide the address publicly; show only the service area.

**Rationale:** solo founder without commercial office; lawn-care is
inherently field-based; visible residential address exposes the founder to
unwanted walk-ups and is widely viewed as a weakness by Pinellas customers;
SAB profiles still rank in local-pack for nearby ZIPs and let us choose
which areas to target first.

---

## The two options, side by side

| | Visible address (storefront) | Service-area business (SAB) |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer sees** | Street address on profile | Only "Serves Largo + 5 ZIPs" |
| **Google Maps marker** | Yes (red pin) | No (only service-area ring) |
| **Local-pack ranking** | Same — both eligible | Same — both eligible |
| **Verification method** | Postcard to address | Postcard or phone/SMS to address (still needs real address) |
| **Walk-ins accepted** | Implied by Google | Explicitly "no" by config |
| **Founder's privacy** | Low — visible | High — hidden |
| **Best for** | Storefronts, shops, restaurants | Solo operators, contractors, mobile services |
| **Risk** | "Why is this guy's house on Google?" | Google sometimes downgrades SAB in 1-pack for non-mobile services |
| **Cost** | $0 | $0 (PO box optional, $5-15/mo) |

## Why SAB for Largo Lawn

1. **No commercial office.** The charter says "Solo founder / lean operating
   model" (D-0004). Renting office space pre-revenue is a charter violation.
2. **Lawn care is mobile.** SAB is the GBP-blessed category for
   contractors without a customer-facing location.
3. **Pinellas customer expectations.** Customers hiring a lawn service do
   NOT expect to drive to a lawn office — they expect the service to come
   to them. Hiding the address matches the expectation.
4. **Solo-founder privacy.** Visible residential address invites walk-ups
   during mow hours, surprise visits, and home exposure.
5. **Selective area visibility.** SAB lets us list the six ZIPs we actually
   serve and rank in local-pack for each. Visible-address profiles only
   rank near the pin.

## The verification step (the gotcha)

Google still requires a **real, deliverable address** to send the
verification postcard to. Even SAB profiles need it.

Options:

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder's residential address** | $0; immediate | Founder's home address becomes the business registration on Sunbiz, and may be searchable via FL public records |
| **CMRA / virtual mailbox (e.g., iPostal1, AnytimeMailbox)** | $10-15/mo; real street address; mail forwarding; privacy | Adds a recurring cost (deferred until OBJ-M2-001 fires at $500 revenue gate) |
| **PO Box (UPS Store, USPS)** | $5-15/mo; cheap | PO boxes are **rejected** by Google for GBP verification — must be a street address |

**Recommended at M0 (right now):** use the founder's residential address
for GBP verification. When M2 fires and the LLC files (OBJ-M2-001), upgrade
to a UPS Store street-address mailbox ($15/mo) for ongoing privacy.

## What to enter in the GBP "Service area" field

Six ZIPs, in priority order:

1. **33771** (Largo — primary)
2. **33770** (Largo)
3. **33778** (Largo / Seminole border)
4. **33773** (Largo)
5. **33774** (Largo)
6. **33756** (Clearwater — adjacent, dense)

In GBP UI: set "Service area" → "Largo, FL" + each ZIP code individually.
Do NOT set cities outside the 6 ZIPs. If we expand service area, edit
later.

## Profile configuration that maximizes local-pack rank for SAB

1. **Primary category:** "Lawn care service"
2. **Secondary categories:** "Landscaper", "Pressure washing service"
   (skip if not offered at launch), "Tree service"
3. **Hide address:** YES (set during GBP creation; cannot be set after the
   first verification if visible-address was chosen)
4. **Service area:** six ZIPs above
5. **Hours:** M–Sat 7:00–17:00 (set before launch; GBP requires it)
6. **Phone:** (727) 555-0123 — must be a local number, not toll-free, not
   the founder's personal cell unless that's OK
7. **Website:** largolawn.pro (or placeholder until domain purchased)
8. **Booking URL:** largolawn.pro/quote (after /quote page ships)
9. **Messaging:** enabled (turn on Google Business Messages)

## Decisions deferred until after M1 (5 jobs completed)

These do NOT block GBP creation but inform post-launch tweaks:

- **Whether to upgrade to a CMRA mailbox** when LLC files (M2 gate, ~$500
  revenue trigger). Adds $15/mo. Worth it once LLC exists to keep home
  address off public records.
- **Whether to add a service-area exclusion** (e.g., exclude a problematic
  neighborhood). Local SEO best practice but not needed at launch.
- **Whether to register a second GBP** for hurricane-prep specifically.
  Not recommended — single GBP with strong categories beats split profiles.

---

## What this doc is NOT

Not a license. Not legal advice. Pinellas County and City of Largo have
home-based-business allowances that permit SAB-style operations; the
specific rules differ by zoning. Confirm with the city before registration
if the founder's residential property is in a non-conforming zone.
