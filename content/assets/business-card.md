# Business Card — Print Spec

> **Print:** Vistaprint 500-card standard matte, $20.
> **When to order:** After pilot #3 closes, when cash covers the order.
> **Until then:** Digital card (this spec) lives in `/preview/assets/business-card`.

## Specs

- **Size:** 3.5" × 2" (US standard)
- **Stock:** 16pt matte cover (heavy, premium feel)
- **Corners:** Square (no rounded — looks more confident)
- **Color:** Two-sided

## Front (cream background)

```
[logo-mark.svg centered, 0.4" diameter, top 0.3"]

YOUR NEIGHBOR'S LAWN MOWER

[space]

Largo Lawn
```

- Logo mark at top.
- Tagline centered, brand-ll-green, Inter 9pt, letter-spacing 0.05em.
- "Largo Lawn" wordmark below, Inter 18pt, bold.

- "YOUR NEIGHBORHOOD LAWN CARE" headline, Inter 11pt, bold, ll-green.

- Contact block, Inter 11pt.

## Why this design

- **Two-sided but no fluff.** Front = brand. Back = what you do + how to reach you.
- **No taglines like "Licensed & Insured"** (we're not, yet — brand guideline anti-brand).
- **No email signature block.** Phone + email + URL is enough.
- **Tagline above the wordmark** so anyone glancing recognizes the brand promise before reading the name.
