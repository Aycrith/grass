# Brand Guidelines — Largo Lawn

> **Purpose:** Single source of truth for how Largo Lawn shows up in
> every customer-facing surface — website, GBP, citations, social,
> print, phone, email, SMS. Anything not in this doc is unbranded.
> **Authority:** This doc. When in doubt, defer here.
> **Last updated:** 2026-07-10

---

## Brand promise (one sentence)

> **Local lawn care that's actually local — one person, one truck, every yard.**

Three things this is doing:
- **"Local"** twice — emphasizes the only durable competitive advantage
  a solo founder has against regional/national operators
- **"One person, one truck, every yard"** — concrete and accountable;
  no corporate ambiguity
- **"Actually"** — implies the market is full of fake-local operators
  (national chains with local-sounding names); differentiation by sincerity

## Voice (how we sound)

**We sound like:** a neighbor who happens to be really good at lawn
care. Plain-spoken. Direct. Not slick.

**Voice attributes (ranked):**
1. **Plain** — short words, short sentences. No marketing-speak.
2. **Honest** — admit limits. "I don't do fertilization" is a feature.
3. **Local** — references to 33771, Pinellas, the heat, the rain.
4. **Warm** — first-name basis, friendly follow-ups, handwritten
   thank-you notes.
5. **Practical** — focus on outcomes (clean cut, clean edges), not
   process (we use commercial-grade X).

**Voice sample — what we'd say vs. what we wouldn't:**

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Industry-leading lawn care solutions" | "I mow lawns." |
| "Satisfaction guaranteed" | "If you're not happy, I'll come back." |
| "Comprehensive landscaping services" | "Mowing, edging, blowing — and mulching when you ask." |
| "Serving the greater Largo area" | "I'm in 33771. You probably see my truck." |
| "Contact us today for a free estimate" | "Text me at 727-XXX-XXXX. I'll be there this week." |
| "Our team of professionals" | "Just me." |

## Tone (how it shifts by surface)

| Surface | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Website (hero, services) | Confident, plain | "Weekly mowing in Largo. $55-95/visit." |
| GBP description | Helpful, local | "Local lawn care in 33771. Same-week quotes." |
| Phone (inbound) | Warm, efficient | "Hey, this is [name]. Yeah, I can come take a look — when works?" |
| Email | Slightly formal, clear | Subject: "Your quote — 123 Main St" |
| SMS | Casual, brief | "On my way. 10 min. — [name]" |
| Door hanger | Direct, low-pressure | "Need your lawn mowed? Text 727-XXX-XXXX. First mow $25 off." |
| Review-request | Grateful, not demanding | "Thanks for trusting me with your yard. A 30-sec Google review means a lot." |
| Weather cancellation | Apologetic, no-charge | "Rain check — literally. We'll reschedule, no fee." |
| Bad-news (damage, complaint) | Accountable, no excuses | "That was my mistake. I'll fix it tomorrow at no charge." |

## Color palette

Five colors. Every surface uses these. No exceptions without explicit update here.

| Token | Hex | Usage | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ll-green` | `#1F4E2C` | Primary, headers, logo | Deep forest — natural, professional, Florida-friendly (not lime/cliché) |
| `--ll-sand` | `#D4A574` | Secondary, accents, warm highlights | Florida earth — approachable, distinguishes from competitor green-overload |
| `--ll-sky` | `#3B7DD8` | Links, CTAs, trust signals | Blue signals trust/water — use sparingly, never on >20% of a page |
| `--ll-charcoal` | `#1A1A1A` | Body text, high-contrast | Near-black for readability |
| `--ll-cream` | `#FAF6F0` | Backgrounds, card surfaces | Warm off-white, easier on eyes than pure white |

**Don't:**
- ❌ Lime green / neon green (cliché lawn-care)
- ❌ Black + neon yellow (construction-equipment vibe)
- ❌ Pure white backgrounds (clinical, not local)
- ❌ More than 3 colors on a single surface

## Typography

Two fonts. Both freely available (Google Fonts).

| Role | Font | Weight | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings | **Inter** | 700 (Bold) | `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif` |
| Body | **Inter** | 400 (Regular) | same |
| Numeric / pricing | **Inter** | 600 (SemiBold) — tabular figures | same |

Single font family (Inter) keeps the brand cohesive. The variable
font supports weights 100-900 with no performance hit.

**Type scale:**
- Hero: 48px / 56px line-height (mobile 32px / 40px)
- H1: 36px / 44px (mobile 28px / 36px)
- H2: 28px / 36px (mobile 22px / 30px)
- H3: 22px / 30px
- Body: 16px / 24px (1.5 line-height for readability)
- Small: 14px / 20px

**Don't:**
- ❌ Script fonts (gimmicky for service businesses)
- ❌ Serif fonts (corporate, dated)
- ❌ All-caps body text (shouting)
- ❌ More than 2 font sizes per surface

## Logo

Two variants. Both live at `brand/logo.svg` and `brand/logo-mark.svg`.

- **Wordmark** (`logo.svg`) — "Largo Lawn" with grass-blade accent on the "L"
- **Mark only** (`logo-mark.svg`) — grass-blade "L" mark for favicons, GBP
  profile photo, business card corner

**Logo usage rules:**
- Always render at >= 80px wide (digital) or >= 1 inch (print)
- Always use `--ll-green` for the wordmark on light backgrounds
- Always use `--ll-cream` for the wordmark on dark backgrounds
- Minimum clear-space = height of the "L" on all sides
- ❌ Don't skew, rotate, recolor outside the palette, or add drop shadows

## Photography style guide

**The brand is the work.** Photos of actual lawns (not stock) build
trust faster than any copy.

**Photo requirements:**
- **Before/after pairs** — side-by-side, same angle, same time-of-day
- **Phone-camera is fine** — natural > polished for local services
- **Lighting:** morning (7-9am) or late afternoon (4-6pm) — avoid
  harsh noon shadows
- **Subject:** the lawn, mostly. Person (you) optional, in branded
  shirt if available.
- **Editing:** minimal. Slight contrast boost OK. Don't add filters.

**Required GBP photos:**
- **Profile photo** — your face, plain background, friendly (use the
  logo mark if you'd rather stay semi-anonymous)
- **Cover photo** — your best before/after, 1024×576px
- **Work photos** — at least 5, ideally 10+, all before/after pairs

## What we don't do (anti-brand)

These are the things we never say, never show, never promise:

- ❌ "Eco-friendly" (meaningless marketing term)
- ❌ "Licensed & insured" (until we actually are — deferred items)
- ❌ "Free estimate" (we don't estimate, we quote — and we charge
  for landscape design work)
- ❌ "Satisfaction guaranteed" (we fix problems; we don't guarantee
  outcomes)
- ❌ "Family-owned" (it's just you)
- ❌ Before/after photos of other people's lawns (without written
  permission)
- ❌ Reviews/ratings from non-customers
- ❌ Promotional language on GBP ("#1 lawn care in Largo!" — Google
  penalizes)

## Brand consistency checklist

Before publishing any customer-facing artifact, verify:

- [ ] Colors are from the palette (no off-palette hex codes)
- [ ] Font is Inter (no other font families)
- [ ] Voice is plain, honest, local (not corporate)
- [ ] NAP matches `largolawn.pro` exactly (name, address, phone, domain)
- [ ] No "licensed & insured" claims until OBJ-M2-002/003 reactive
- [ ] No before/after photos without written permission
- [ ] Tagline (if used) matches the chosen default
