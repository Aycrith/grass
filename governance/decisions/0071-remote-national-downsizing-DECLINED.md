## 0. The decision in one paragraph

Record REMOTE_NATIONAL_DOWNSIZING (national senior downsizing & move management, remote coordinator + local-contractor network, serving out-of-state family of FL seniors) as **DECLINED** under the v6 ratification-reset synthesis (2026-08-01).

**Rationale (structural refutation precedes gap-closing):** The v5 finding that 5+ named NASMM (National Association of Senior Move Managers) operators already serve Pinellas/Tampa Bay is a primary-source competitive enumeration, NOT a hypothesis. This directly refutes the out-of-state-family-of-FL-seniors underserved thesis on which the lane's selection rested. The 5 gap-closing paths (NASMM State of the Industry ~$200-$500, Caring Transitions FDD Item 19 via qualified franchise applicant, 90-day sole-operator SMM outreach, ALF/geriatric-care-manager interviews, multi-state nexus matrix) are TAM/margin/revenue verifications — none of them changes competitive density.

**Q3 + Q5 both fail independently:** the lane requires multi-state subcontractor network + 12-month warm-pipeline ramp + multi-state foreign qualification ($100-$300/yr × 9 states = $900-$2,700/yr fixed) — which are distribution-pathway dependencies BAKED INTO the unit economics, not separable. Per-engagement revenue at the sole-operator level is structurally capped by existing operator capture; per-engagement revenue is UNVERIFIED at the $30/hr threshold without acquiring a paid NASMM State of Industry report + 90-day interview pipeline + franchise-intent FDD probe (none scheduled or funded).

**Reopen requires ALL THREE structural conditions** (none currently met) AND a separately ratified Decision Template.
