# Runbook 4 — Equipment Access Plan

> **Use:** Reference when something breaks, when you're buying gear, or when the truck needs reorganizing.
> **Goal:** Solo operator has every tool within 90 seconds of reach, even when tired, even in the dark.
> **Cash gate:** Equipment upgrades gated by `state/ledger.yaml → OBJ-M2-005` ($5,000 trigger). Day-1 setup: under $1,500.

## Day-1 starter kit (under $1,500, pre-Month-1)

### Mower

**Push mower (entry level)**:
- Honda HRX217VKA — $700. Self-propelled, variable speed, 21" deck. Best residential mower on the market. Survives 5+ years of weekly use.
- Alternative: Toro 21466 SmartStow — $550. Cheaper, slightly less reliable.
- **Don't buy a riding mower yet.** Wait until you have 8+ weekly customers with lots over 1/4 acre.

### String trimmer

- Stihl FS 56 RC-E — $250. The trimmer that every solo operator ends up with. Gas-powered, 2-cycle, easy to start.
- Alternative: Echo SRM-225 — $230. Slightly lighter, same quality.

## Truck organization

The truck bed is your workshop. Every minute you spend looking for a tool is a minute you don't bill.

### Bed layout (left-to-right looking from the back)

```
+---------+---------+---------+---------+
|  BLOWER | EDGER   | MOWER   |  OPEN   |
|         |         | (front) |  SPACE  |
+---------+---------+---------+---------+
                                          ↑
                              Fuel can strapped here
```

### Storage box (weatherproof, mounted on front wall of bed)

```
+-----------------------+
|  Hand tools           |
|  Pruners, loppers,    |
|  saw, gloves          |
+-----------------------+
|  Safety gear          |
|  Glasses, plugs,      |
|  first-aid kit        |
+-----------------------+
|  Trim line spares     |
+-----------------------+
|  Tarps (rolled)       |
+-----------------------+
|  Trash bags, towels   |
+-----------------------+
```

### Why this layout

- **Mower goes in first / comes out last.** It's the heaviest. Driver-side ramp access.
- **Edger + blower mid-bed.** Easy reach from either side.
- **Open space for green waste.** Clippings, branches, hedge debris. Throw in, dump at the day's end.
- **Tools accessible without moving equipment.** The storage box is mounted at chest height, not buried.

## Maintenance schedule

### Weekly (every Sunday evening, 30 minutes)

- Mower: blade check, air filter, oil level.
- Trimmer: line replacement, spark plug check.
- Edger: blade check, lubing.
- Blower: air filter, fuel line check.
- Truck: tire pressure, fluid levels, clean out bed.

### Monthly (first Sunday of month, 1 hour)

- Mower blade sharpening or replacement.
- Trimmer line spool replacement.
- Edger blade replacement (every 3 months — these wear fast).
- Equipment deep clean.
- Truck oil change (every 3 months or 3,000 mi).

### Quarterly

- Replace trimmer spark plug.
- Replace edger blade.
- Replace blower air filter.
- Sharpen or replace mower blade.
- Replace work gloves if worn.

### Annually

- Replace starter cords (if frayed).
- Replace air filters on all 2-cycle equipment.
- Replace mower blade (or sharpen twice — once at 6mo).
- Replace work boots (if worn).
- Truck inspection.

## Equipment failure playbook

### Mower won't start

1. Fuel? (most common cause — top off)
2. Spark plug? (remove, check for wet fouling, replace if black/sooty)
3. Air filter? (clean or replace)
4. Choke? (cold start = full choke)
5. If none of above: take to small-engine repair. Don't lose a full day trying to fix a $700 mower. Most repairs are $50-150.

### Trimmer line keeps breaking

1. Wrong line diameter? Check manufacturer spec.
2. Line is old/dry? Buy fresh.
3. Cutting too close to hardscape? Back off 1/4".
4. Spool worn? Replace ($10).

### Blower won't start

1. Same as mower — fuel, plug, filter.
2. 2-cycle mix right? (50:1 typically)
3. Pull cord stuck? Inspect recoil.

### Lost or stolen equipment

- Yard-sign theft is ~5% / month. Don't chase.
- Truck break-in is rare but possible. Don't leave valuables in the cab overnight.
- Insurance (when active, post-Month 3) covers theft. Until then, the cost is on you.

## When to upgrade

| Trigger | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 8+ weekly customers with > 1/4 acre lots | Riding mower ($2,500-3,500) |
| 12+ weekly customers | 2-man crew (second operator, $25/hr) |
| 20+ weekly customers | Truck upgrade (enclosed trailer) |
| 30+ weekly customers | Second truck + dedicated equipment |
| 50+ weekly customers | You're a real business, not a side hustle. Pivot to commercial contracts. |

**Don't pre-buy.** Each upgrade happens when the trigger metric is hit. Pre-buying riding mowers and trailers before you have the revenue is how solo operators go bankrupt in Q2.

## Storage at home

- **Indoor storage preferred.** Garage, shed, or covered patio. Mower + trimmer + edger last 50% longer indoors.
- **Fuel storage:** 5 gallons max at home, in approved container, away from house. Local fire code.
- **Trimmer line + spares:** dry storage. Humidity makes line brittle.
- **Tarps:** dry storage. Folded, not crumpled.

---

## The "what if I lost everything" insurance

Photograph all equipment annually. Save to Google Drive `/equipment/photos/`. List serial numbers. This is how insurance claims get paid fast (post-Month 3) and how you remember what you owned if there's a fire or theft.
