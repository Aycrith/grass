## Field 3: Address strategy (PICK ONE)

This determines which verification flow applies.

### Option A — Service-Area Business (SAB) — RECOMMENDED

- Hide the physical address from the GBP profile.
- Set service area ZIPs: 33771, 33770, 33778, 33773, 33774, 33756.
- Verification postcard goes to the steward's home address (mailbox required).
- Pros: no customer visits to a home office; matches actual operating model
  (mobile-only, no storefront).
- Cons: slight local-pack ranking penalty (~5-10%) vs. visible-address
  businesses.

### Option B — Visible address (if steward has a commercial location)

- Show full street address on GBP.
- Verification postcard to that address.
- Pros: small local-pack ranking boost.
- Cons: requires a real commercial address; not applicable to most
  solo-founder launch scenarios.

### Option C — Hybrid (co-working mailbox service)

- Use a real commercial mailbox address (e.g., UPS Store, Pak Mail) for
  GBP listing.
- Pros: real address visible, no home exposure.
- Cons: monthly cost ($20-50); some GBP reviewers flag commercial
  mailboxes; slightly slower verification.

**Recommended: Option A** (Service-Area Business). Matches the operating
model (mobile crew, no storefront), avoids the home-address exposure, and
the ~5-10% ranking penalty is more than offset by the 6-ZIP service-area
coverage and citation-burst workstream.

## Field 5: Phone

Use a **dedicated Google Voice number** (free) for the GBP. Do NOT use the
steward's personal cell. Reasons:

- Call recording for QA / dispute resolution
- After-hours voicemail → email transcription
- Number portability if business is later sold
- Separate personal/business identity

Setup steps:
1. Create Google Voice account at https://voice.google.com (free with any
   Google account).
2. Pick a number with a **727 area code** (Pinellas County) — not 813 or
   352. Local area code is a GBP ranking signal.
3. Set voicemail transcription to email delivery.
4. Use this number on GBP, every citation, and the website contact page.

## Q&A — pre-seed (GBP "Questions & Answers" tab)

Google lets the business owner ASK and ANSWER their own questions on GBP.
Pre-seeding this section is the most underused GBP ranking tactic. Ask
these 5 questions from the steward's account, then answer them:

**Q1:** "Do you offer weekly service in Largo FL?"
**A1:** "Yes — weekly and bi-weekly residential mowing is our most
popular service. Visit pinellaslawn.com for pricing or message us here
for a free quote."

**Q2:** "Are you licensed and insured?"
**A2:** "Yes. We carry $1M general liability insurance and workers' comp
exemption filing on file. License numbers available on request."

**Q3:** "What areas do you serve?"
**A3:** "Largo (33771) and surrounding Pinellas County communities —
Belleair, Harbor Bluffs, Ridgecrest, Indian Rocks, and the 33770/33773/
33774/33778/33756 corridor. Free quotes for properties in these ZIPs."

**Q4:** "Do you offer one-time cleanups?"
**A4:** "Yes — seasonal cleanups, hurricane prep, and one-time mulching
are all available as single visits. We also offer recurring weekly
service."

**Q5:** "How quickly can I get a quote?"
**A5:** "Most quotes returned within 24 hours of the request. For
hurricane prep during an active storm, same-day response on weekdays
and Saturdays."

(Each Q&A pair should be on the steward's own account, not a customer's.
Self-seeded Q&A from the owner is allowed by GBP ToS.)

## Reviews — request strategy (do NOT fake, do NOT buy)

GBP reviews are the single highest-impact ranking signal after the
business name + category. Plan to acquire 10+ genuine reviews in the
first 90 days via:

### Closed-beta review acquisition (first 5 reviews — Week 2-4)

The 5 paid pilot jobs (per OBJ-M2-006 description) are the
review-acquisition engine. Process for each pilot job:

1. Job completes → send review-request text within 2 hours via the
   steward's Google Voice number (or directly from the steward's cell
   for now — Google Voice tap later).
2. Review-request template:

   > "Thanks for choosing PinellasLawn! If you have 30 seconds, an
   > honest Google review helps us a lot — especially as a new local
   > business. Tap the link to leave one:
   > [GBP review link — generated after profile is verified]
   > Thanks again — [steward first name]"

3. Do NOT promise anything in exchange for the review.
4. Do NOT select which platform (Google vs. Yelp vs. NextDoor).
5. If the customer doesn't leave a review, send ONE follow-up 7 days
   later, then stop. Persistent review solicitation is a GBP ToS
   violation.

### Steady-state review flow (post-pilot)

After Month 1, add a review-request to the automated post-job notification
(workflow `wf_send_review_request`). Target: ≥30% of completed jobs
generate a review within 30 days.
