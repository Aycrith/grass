**Date:** 2026-07-10 | **Owner:** seo agent (with research support) | **Decision dependency:** D-0007 brand-domain

---

## TL;DR

Largo FL has a tractable 100-keyword universe organized into **5 money categories**. The categorical SEO finding: **"Lawn care service" outperforms "Landscaper"** in this market because (a) higher search volume, (b) less franchise competition, (c) GBP category specificity. Mission 1's GBP profile MUST use "Lawn care service" as primary category.

---

## GBP categorical choice (THE critical SEO decision)

| Category | Search volume index | Competition | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Lawn care service"** | High | Medium | ✅ **USE** — primary GBP category |
| "Landscaper" | Medium-High | High (franchises, big crews) | ❌ Avoid as primary |
| "Lawn maintenance service" | Medium | Low | ✅ Use as secondary |
| "Yard care service" | Low-Medium | Low | ✅ Use as secondary |
| "Tree service" | High (separate intent) | High | Out of scope for MVP |
| "Pest control service" | High | Very High (franchises) | Out of scope for MVP |

**Why this matters:** GBP category is the single highest-leverage SEO lever for a local service business. "Landscaper" returns national franchises (Massey, TruGreen, LawnStarter aggregators) on the map pack. "Lawn care service" returns solo operators and small crews — the actual competitive set a solo founder can beat.

## Risks

1. **Brand-name lock-in.** If D-0007 picks a name that doesn't fit any keyword, SEO suffers. Mitigation: name should contain or rhyme with a service word (lawn, yard, green, edge, cut).
2. **GBP suspension for NAP inconsistency.** Mitigation: lock NAP in spreadsheet, audit quarterly, change once if moving.
3. **Google algorithm update.** Mitigation: stay white-hat; no PBN, no link buying.
