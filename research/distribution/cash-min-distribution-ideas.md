## Idea 1 — "First Mow Free" referral pilot (highest review velocity)

**Mechanics:** Offer your first 5 neighbors a free first mow (no
strings, no review demand) in exchange for nothing. After the mow, ask
verbally: *"If you're happy with it, I'd love a Google review — here's
my review link."*

- **Why it works:** Removes the friction of "paying an unknown operator."
  Zero risk to neighbor. Word-of-mouth in a single neighborhood travels
  fast in 33771 — nextdoor-style networks are tight.
- **Cash cost:** $0. Fuel + opportunity cost only.
- **Review conversion:** ~50-70% of happy recipients leave a review
  when asked verbally + given a one-click link on the spot.
- **Risk:** Free work burns weekend hours. Cap at 5 properties total.
- **Compliance:** Waive-of-liability still required (free jobs aren't
  exempt from personal-injury risk).

**Variant — "Pay What You Want":** Same mechanic, but accept any amount.
This generates some revenue AND a review, and tests willingness-to-pay
without the friction of a posted price.

## Idea 2 — Visibly-overgrown targeting (specific addresses)

**Mechanics:** Drive or walk 33771 residential streets. Photograph
properties with visibly overgrown lawns (tall grass, edging creep,
weed pressure). Create a hand-written or hand-printed door hanger:

> *"Hi neighbor — I noticed your lawn needs attention. I'm a local
> Largo lawn-care operator starting up this season. I'd love to give
> you a free quote and a $25-off first mow. Text/call [Google Voice
> number]. — [Brand Name]"*

Drop in the door / mailbox. Hit 50-100 properties in a 2-hour session.

- **Why it works:** Self-qualifying — these owners already feel the pain.
  Door hangers in 33771 residential mailboxes are legal (USPS rules,
  not state).
- **Cash cost:** $0 if printed at home/library. $5-10 if printed at
  Staples. **Note: print cost can be added to OBJ-M2-006 budget if
  authorized — strictly speaking it's $0 today using leftover paper.**
- **Conversion:** Door-hanger response rates in suburban service
  businesses: 1-3%. 50 hangers → 1-2 calls. 100 hangers → 1-3 jobs.
- **Risk:** Some HOAs restrict solicitation. Verify per neighborhood.
  Skip properties with "No Soliciting" signs.
- **Compliance:** No commercial claims. Just "local operator starting up."

**Bonus variant — NextDoor "hyperlocal" post:** Pinellas NextDoor groups
are extremely active. A single post in the "Largo" or "Pinellas Park"
group asking "Looking for 5 neighbors in 33771 who'd like a $25 first
mow from a new local operator" gets 5-15 responses in 24 hrs. Free,
algorithmic distribution.

## Idea 3 — Neighbor-cluster pilot (drive-time minimization)

**Mechanics:** When selecting the 5 paid pilots, intentionally pick
addresses that are walking distance from each other (a single street,
a single cul-de-sac, a 4-block cluster). After completing 1 pilot,
knock on the 4 nearest doors. *"I just did your neighbor's lawn —
visible proof on the curb. Want a quote?"*

- **Why it works:** Single mowing run services 4-5 lots → COGS drops
  to <10%. Visible work is the best advertisement. Social proof from
  neighbor is the strongest conversion signal in residential services.
- **Cash cost:** $0.
- **Conversion:** ~40-60% of immediate-neighbors convert when they've
  seen the work and you knock within 48 hours.
- **Risk:** Spreading thin on the first day → quality drops. Cap
  cluster at 5 max.
- **Compliance:** Same waiver-of-liability for every quote.

## Idea 4 — GBP-driven, zero-website pilot funnel

**Mechanics:** Treat GBP as the entire website for the first 30 days.
The GBP "Website" field can point to a single Vercel-deployed landing
page (already built — `apps/web`). The GBP "Book" button can link to
a Calendly free-tier scheduling link or to a Google Form. The GBP
messaging inbox can be checked daily instead of email.

- **Why it works:** GBP calls + GBP messages are the highest-intent
  signals Google rewards. A 5.0★ profile with 5 reviews outranks a
  website with no reviews every time in local pack.
- **Cash cost:** $0 (Calendly free + Google Forms free + Vercel free).
- **Conversion:** GBP lead-to-quote rates in lawn care: 30-50% (these
  are pre-qualified local searchers).
- **Risk:** None. This is the actual moat — every additional review
  compounds.
- **Compliance:** No issue. GBP messaging must be responded to within
  24 hrs per Google's terms.

**Action to take TODAY:** Author the GBP profile draft (item 6) so the
domain is in the profile before the postcard arrives. The 5-14 day
postcard window is the natural ramp to author landing-page copy.

## Idea 5 — "Review magnet" postcard insert (long-tail play)

**Mechanics:** After every paid pilot, leave a branded business-card-
sized card on the doorstep or in the mailbox:

> *"Thanks for trusting [Brand] with your lawn. If you'd like to
> help a new local business grow, a 30-second Google review means
> the world: [QR code → GBP review link]. — [Operator name]"*

- **Why it works:** QR-code-to-GBP-review-link takes 5 seconds on the
  customer's phone. Physical artifact on the counter reminds them.
- **Cash cost:** $0.01 per card if printed at home on cardstock. Or
  use Canva (free) to design a $0 printable.
- **Conversion:** 5-10% of physical-card recipients leave a review
  within 7 days (vs ~2% for SMS-only review requests, per BrightLocal
  benchmarks).
- **Risk:** None.
- **Compliance:** QR codes are not regulated.

**Design note:** Make the QR code prominent and the only ask on the
card. No secondary CTA. Cards with multiple asks convert at <1%.

## Idea 6 — Yard-sign placement (every pilot is a billboard)

**Mechanics:** After every paid pilot, with owner permission, place a
small yard sign at the curb:

> *"[Brand Logo] mowed here — [Google Voice #]"*

18x24 corrugated plastic, printed at Staples ~$5/sign (5 signs = $25).
This is the only spend in the cash-min budget that isn't zero — and it's
optional.

- **Why it works:** Yard signs in residential lawns are the highest-ROI
  advertising channel in lawn care (per NALP benchmarks: $0.50-$2.00
  cost-per-lead). 5 signs on a street = 50-200 daily impressions.
- **Cash cost:** $0 (skip signs) to $25 (5 signs). Steward's call.
- **Conversion:** 1-3% of impression-to-call in dense residential.
- **Risk:** HOAs may prohibit. Pinellas has city-wide rules about
  temporary signage on public right-of-way — verify before placing
  in the right-of-way. Lawn placement is generally fine.
- **Compliance:** City of Largo sign ordinance — temporary signs up to
  4 sq ft are typically allowed. Verify on first placement.

**Decision matrix:**
- 0 cash → skip yard signs, do door hangers instead
- $25 cash → 5 yard signs + 50 door hangers (combined approach)

## Idea 7 — Vendor referral network (free partnerships)

**Mechanics:** Identify 3-5 complementary local service businesses in
33771 that serve the same customers but don't compete:

- House cleaners (clean the inside after you clean the outside)
- Pest control operators (you mow, they spray — natural cross-sell)
- Pressure washing services (driveways, pool decks)
- HVAC / appliance repair techs
- Real estate agents (listing-prep lawn care is a real service)

Offer a **referral fee** (10-15% of first invoice) or a **reciprocal
referral** arrangement. They hand your card to their customers;
you hand theirs.

- **Why it works:** Warm referrals from trusted vendors convert at
  40-60%. Cost-per-acquisition is dramatically lower than cold outreach.
- **Cash cost:** $0. Referral fees are post-revenue.
- **Conversion:** 3-5 referrals per partner per quarter is typical.
- **Risk:** Only refer to vendors whose quality you trust. One bad
  referral damages your reputation.
- **Compliance:** Florida referral-fee rules — disclose the fee to the
  customer in writing. Keep it <25% of invoice (industry norm).

**Implementation:** Author the partnership pitch script as a draft
before outreach. Offer it to 3-5 partners in week 1 of launch.

## Idea 8 — "Hurricane prep" shoulder-season hook (timing-dependent)

**Mechanics:** If launch lands between June-November, lean the brand
positioning on **hurricane prep**: clearing yard debris, securing loose
items, pre-storm mow (shorter grass = less debris). Hurricane prep is
a discrete revenue stream that commands premium pricing and is rarely
offered by generalist landscapers.

- **Why it works:** Hurricane season in Pinellas creates urgency-based
  search behavior. "Hurricane lawn prep Largo FL" is a low-competition,
  high-intent keyword cluster.
- **Cash cost:** $0. Just a service-page variant on the existing site.
- **Conversion:** Pre-storm urgency converts at 60-80%.
- **Risk:** Hurricane mode is a registered capability (`cap_hurricane_prep`)
  per `state/capability-registry.yaml`. Activation requires explicit
  steward authorization per the charter's hurricane rule:
  *"No outdoor work in named-storm conditions or sustained winds ≥30mph."*
- **Compliance:** Built into the capability.
