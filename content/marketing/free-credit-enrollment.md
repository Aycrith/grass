# Free-Credit Enrollment Package — $1,050+ in ad credits, $0 out of pocket

> **Use:** This is a checklist the steward walks through ONCE, claiming free
> advertising credits from four platforms. After this is done, paid ads run
> at $0 net spend for 30-90 days depending on platform.
> **Constraint:** no credit card required at enrollment on any platform;
> credit card required only when ad actually serves (and we cap spend at
> $5/day).

## STEP 1 — Google Ads ($500 free credit)

### Eligibility
- New Google Ads accounts only
- Must spend $500 within 60 days to redeem the credit
- Once $500 spent, $500 credit posts back; can be used for any ads after that
- Net cash out: **$0** (you spend $500 of credit, then get $500 back)

### Enrollment steps
1. Go to: `https://ads.google.com/home/campaign/`
2. Sign in with the GBP-linked Google account (recommended for NAP consistency)
3. Click "Switch to Expert Mode" (required for credit eligibility)
4. Create a Smart or Search campaign (skip if not ready; can pause)
5. Set daily budget: **$5/day** (so the $500 lasts 100 days)
6. Enter billing details (credit card required to start; we cap at $5/day)
7. After first $500 spent, the credit auto-applies; net effect = free

### URLs to bookmark
- Main: https://ads.google.com/home/campaign/
- Coupon help: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6152251
- $500 promo: typically auto-applied; check "Billing > Promotions"

### Time to complete
**15 minutes.** No campaign launch required (can stay paused).

## STEP 2 — Microsoft Advertising ($500 free credit)

### Eligibility
- New accounts only
- $500 credit upon spending $500 within first 90 days
- Net cash out: **$0**

### Enrollment steps
1. Go to: `https://ads.microsoft.com/`
2. Sign up with the GBP-linked Microsoft account (or create one)
3. Import Google Ads campaign directly (free tool — preserves all settings)
4. Set daily budget: **$5/day** (so the $500 lasts 100 days)
5. Enter billing details
6. Wait for $500-spend threshold; credit auto-applies

### Why this matters even though Google dominates
- Bing serves 5-10% of US search; older demographic, higher household income
- Less competition = cheaper CPC in lawn-care niche
- "Bing people" tend to convert higher (less tire-kicker traffic)

### Time to complete
**15 minutes.**

## STEP 3 — Meta Ads ($50+ free credit)

### Eligibility
- New Meta Business Manager accounts
- Credit varies ($50-$250 typically; not guaranteed)
- Net cash out: **$0**

### Enrollment steps
1. Go to: https://business.facebook.com/
2. Create a Business Manager
3. Set up a Page for "Largo Lawn" (use brand assets)
4. Ads Manager → Create Ad → choose objective (lead form or messages)
5. Set daily budget: **$3/day** (micro-budget; just to validate the channel)
6. Apply promo code (often auto-populates for new accounts)

### Reality check
Meta Ads have **lower intent** for lawn care than Google/Bing (people don't
go to Facebook to find a lawn mower). Useful only as a brand-awareness
side-channel; expected conversions: 1-2 per month at $3/day.

### Time to complete
**20 minutes.**

---

## STEP 4 — Yelp Ads (free months + credit)

### Eligibility
- New advertisers on Yelp
- First month free + first $300 spend credited
- Net cash out: **$0** for first month

### Enrollment steps
1. Claim the GBP-linked Yelp Business Page at https://biz.yelp.com/
2. After 7-14 days of having a claimed profile, an "Upgrade to Ads" offer
   appears in the dashboard
3. Click the offer; first month is free
4. Set monthly budget: **$50/mo** (low; the $300 credit lasts 6 months)
5. **Calendar reminder to cancel before month 7** — otherwise auto-bills
   $200-400/mo

### Reality check
Yelp Ads work for **higher-ticket services** (plumbers, HVAC, roofers)
where a single job pays for months of ads. For $48 mows, the math is
marginal. Recommended budget: $50/mo or less, monitor weekly.

### Time to complete
**10 minutes** + 7-14 days wait for claim email.

## STEP 5 — NextDoor Local Deal (free first post)

### Eligibility
- NextDoor Business Page (free)
- "Local Deal" first post is free in some ZIPs

### Enrollment steps
1. Create a Business Page at https://business.nextdoor.com/
2. Verify the business address
3. Post a "Local Deal": "Free first mow for the first 5 neighbors who text me"
4. Optional: $30-50/zip to promote the post (skip initially)

### Time to complete
**20 minutes.**

## STEP 6 — Thumbtack (free first leads)

### Eligibility
- New Thumbtack pro account
- Free first N leads (varies by category; lawn care typically 5-10 free)

### Enrollment steps
1. Go to: https://www.thumbtack.com/pro
2. Sign up as a Pro
3. Complete profile (services, photos, ZIPs)
4. Get matched with leads; first 5-10 are free; subsequent leads $5-15 each

### Reality check
Thumbtack is lead-gen; you pay for contact info. Useful as one channel
in the mix, not a primary. Budget: $50/mo leads cap.

### Time to complete
**30 minutes** (profile takes longer than enrollment).
