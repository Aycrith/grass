         (Florida single-member LLC, to be formed)

Principal: $15,000 (fifteen thousand US dollars)

Disbursement: Lump sum to Largo Lawn LLC business bank account
             within 5 business days of LLC formation

  - 50% of monthly free cash flow applied to outstanding principal
  - "Free cash flow" = net profit after tax reserve (25%) and
    equipment replacement reserve (10%)
  - Monthly payments begin Month 3 (after initial operating reserve
    is built)
  - Full amortization expected by Month 24 under baseline scenario

Security: Unsecured. Borrower is the personal obligor.

Reporting:
  - Monthly: 1-page email update by 10th of following month
  - Quarterly: P&L statement by 15th of following month
  - Annually: In-person or video review meeting

Mid-term review (Month 12):
  - If principal not reduced by ≥50%, parties discuss restructuring.

Prepayment: Borrower may pre-pay in full or in part at any time
           without penalty.

Default: 90 days missed payment → loan acceleration. Lender may
         demand immediate repayment of outstanding principal.

Default cure: Borrower may propose restructuring (extended term,
              reduced payments) which Lender may accept or reject
              in writing within 30 days.

Governing law: Florida
